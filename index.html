Lead Generation

A private outreach assistant for a single user.

Lead Generation is a personal tool used by its owner to find potential business contacts, prepare personalised emails, send them from the owner's own Gmail account, and keep track of replies so that conversations can be continued.

The application runs locally on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and does not allow other people to sign in or connect their accounts.

See the Privacy Policy for how Gmail data is used.
